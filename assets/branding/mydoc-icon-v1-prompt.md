# MyDoc 图标 v1

使用内置 image_gen 生成。主文件：`mydoc-icon-v1.png`。

设计方向：沿用应用克莱因蓝，用白色折叠纸页构成 M 形，表达文档收藏与创作；圆角方形底板，外部透明。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished square desktop application icon for MyDoc, a local-first Markdown document library with a whiteboard.
Primary request: Design one distinctive, beautifully precise app icon. A single bold ivory-white geometric symbol formed from two overlapping folded document sheets, their negative space subtly suggesting an uppercase M. It must first read as a memorable paper/document mark, with at most two large sheet planes and one small folded corner. Strong readable silhouette at 32px.
Style: restrained Swiss graphic design, clean vector-like edges, refined proportions, minimal, very subtle paper depth only.
Composition: one centered icon, straight-on, large mark occupying 60 percent of tile width, ample even padding. A rich Klein blue #002FA7 rounded-square tile occupies 88 percent of the square image, leaving genuinely transparent space outside it. Smooth rounded corners, modest radius, no external drop shadow.
Palette: Klein blue tile and warm ivory-white mark, a slightly darker blue plane if needed for fold distinction.
Constraints: deliver ONLY the single app icon, high resolution square. No letters, no typography, no wordmark, no captions, no mockup, no surrounding scene, no grids, no extra symbols, no sparkles, no pen, no circuits, no watermark. The M should be suggested by paper geometry rather than typeset. Preserve a real transparent alpha outside the tile.
